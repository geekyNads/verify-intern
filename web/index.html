VerifyIntern

A public, source-backed register of reported internship and job scams. Paste an offer you've received, or type a company name or website, and see whether anything in it matches a documented scam pattern.

Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored. The check runs inside your browser, against the dataset in this repository.

Report a scam you've run into

Reports open a public GitHub issue for a maintainer to review against the evidence you give. Nothing is added to the register automatically, and nothing here names a private individual — flags attach to companies, domains and patterns.
